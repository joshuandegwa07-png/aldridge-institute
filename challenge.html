Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. Every course and certificate is free through December 31, 2026, with no card required. After that, courses return to normal pricing, except for Founding Members and pilot testers, who keep free access as described above.

How do recruiters know a certificate is real?

Every certificate has a unique ID and a public verification page on aldridgeinstitute.com. Anyone can confirm who earned it, which course it covers, and when it was issued.

How long does a course take?

About 30 minutes, including the assessment. You get 5 attempts per course per day.

Who can join?

Anyone. Aldridge is built for college students recruiting for internships and for working professionals building new skills.
