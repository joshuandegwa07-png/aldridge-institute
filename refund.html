Refund Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

All sales are final

Our courses are digital and you get access right away, so we don't offer refunds, credits or exchanges once you've bought something.

Individual courses ($10)

Each course is a one-time purchase. You get access as soon as your payment goes through, and the sale is not refundable.

Unlimited membership ($25/month)

Unlimited renews every month until you cancel. You can cancel anytime through our membership portal, and you keep access until the end of the month you've already paid for. We don't give refunds or partial refunds for months that have already been charged, including if you cancel partway through a month. Once you cancel, you won't be charged again.

Problems with your purchase

We'll make it right if:

  • you paid but can't get into your course,
  • you were charged more than once for the same purchase, or
  • you were charged after you cancelled.

Email us within 14 days of the charge and we'll fix your access or refund the charge that shouldn't have happened.
